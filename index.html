What I do

Retrieval systems

Chunking, hybrid and lexical retrieval, reranking. Getting the right context in front of the model, because retrieval quality is the ceiling on answer quality and no amount of prompting raises it.

Evaluation harnesses

Recall, MRR, faithfulness, abstention — measured separately, because a single blended score hides the failure mode that actually gets a system pulled from production.

Shipping the service

The unglamorous half: typed APIs, graceful degradation, container builds, CI, and a test suite that runs without an API key.

How I work

  1. Define what "working" means

    Before any model call: what does a correct answer look like, and what does a correct refusal look like? Most RAG projects skip the second question and discover it in production.

  2. Measure the baseline first

    A number is meaningless without the trivial alternative beside it. 69% accuracy against a 61.8% majority-class baseline is a very different claim from 69% alone — and only one of them is honest.

  3. Change one thing, re-measure

    Ablations over intuition. If a reranker, a chunking strategy or a bigger model cannot be shown to have helped on a held-out set, it did not earn its latency.
